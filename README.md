# The Human Poster Child

**Posters drawn by an actual human.**

The Human Poster Child is an independent creative initiative dedicated to celebrating human-made poster art and connecting artists with the people, projects, and communities that need them.

In a world increasingly saturated with AI-generated imagery, we believe it is more important than ever to preserve and support the value of art made by people: the imagination, intention and meaning behind every design.

Our mission is simple: **make it easier to discover, support, and commission human artists.**

## Our Vision

Posters have a long history as a medium for artistic expression, cultural identity, grassroots organizing, music, activism, and community building.

From concert posters to restaurant menus and independent event flyers, poster art has always been a space for experimentation and personal expression.

We want to help keep that tradition alive.

The Human Poster Child aims to create a welcoming home for artists working across styles, mediums, and disciplines, united by one principle: **the artwork is made by humans.**

We believe that choosing human-made art is a meaningful way to support creative livelihoods, preserve artistic diversity, and encourage a culture that values the people behind the work.

## What We're Building

The Human Poster Child is envisioned as a growing collection of human-made poster art and a place for artists and potential collaborators to find one another.

The platform aims to offer:

- **A poster gallery:** Discover original designs created by human artists.
- **Artist profiles:** Explore individual artists, their work, and their creative perspectives.
- **Direct connections:** Reach out to artists about commissions, collaborations, and creative opportunities.
- **Community-minded projects:** Help small businesses, independent events, grassroots organizations, and other initiatives find artists who share their values.
- **Accessible collaboration:** Give artists the option to indicate their interest in commercial, independent, or volunteer projects.

Our goal is to keep the experience simple, approachable, and centered on the artists themselves.

## Human-Made by Design

The Human Poster Child champions artwork created through human imagination, skill, and creative decision-making.

We welcome traditional and digital artistic practices, including illustration, painting, collage, typography, vector design, photography, and experimental techniques.

Our focus is not on prescribing a particular aesthetic or medium, nor policing whether AI was ever used in an artist's creative process. It's on celebrating human creations and providing an alternative to generative AI imagery.

**Human creativity isn't a style. It's the point.**

## Project Status

The Human Poster Child is an independent project in its early stages of development.

The initial website will focus on introducing the mission, showcasing artwork, and creating straightforward ways for visitors to discover and contact participating artists.

Additional features and community opportunities may be explored as the project grows.

## Get Involved

Are you an artist who creates original poster designs? Do you organize events, run an independent business, or have a project that could benefit from human-made artwork?

The Human Poster Child is being built for you.

Whether you're looking to share your work, discover new artists, or collaborate on something meaningful, we hope you'll become part of this growing creative community.

## Our Belief

Art is more than an image.

It expresses something that reflects the intentionality and importance of your business's own product.

**Let's keep people in the picture.**
